ENGLISH READING • ゆっくり朗読 (約135 wpm)
Cities Create Public Guarantor Programs for Living-Alone Seniors
SPEED
※文クリックで個別再生・🇯🇵で日本語訳表示

As more elderly people live alone without nearby family members, Japanese cities are offering official support services for their daily lives. Many hospitals and nursing homes require a family guarantor before a patient can be admitted, creating problems for solo seniors.

In the past, some private guarantor companies charged unfairly high fees without providing proper legal care. Under new city programs, local officials and lawyers act as trusted guarantors to help seniors enter hospitals safely. This system ensures that everyone can receive necessary medical care, regardless of whether they have surviving relatives.

【日本語全訳】

身近に家族がおらず一人暮らしをする高齢者が増えるにつれ、日本の各都市は彼らの日常生活のための公的な支援サービスを提供し始めています。 多くの病院や老人ホームは入所前に家族の身元保証人を求めるため、一人暮らしの高齢者にとって問題となっています。

過去には、一部の民間保証会社が適切な法的ケアを提供することなく不当に高い料金を請求していました。 新しい市の制度のもとでは、高齢者が安全に入院できるよう、自治体の担当者や弁護士が信頼できる保証人として活動します。 この仕組みは、生きている親族がいるかどうかにかかわらず、誰もが必要な医療を受けられることを保証しています。

■ 英検準2級〜2級 重要語彙（Vocabulary Drill）

語句・発音 意味・品詞 定義 & 例文
guarantor
 noun
保証人
A person or institution that gives a guarantee to pay debts or fulfill obligations if another person fails to do so.
Her uncle agreed to act as the financial guarantor for her student apartment.
admit
 verb
（病院や施設に）受け入れる、入院させる
To allow someone to enter a hospital, school, or other institution.
The emergency room admitted the injured cyclist immediately.
unfairly
 adv
不当に、不公平に
In a way that is not fair, reasonable, or balanced.
Consumers complained that the company was unfairly overcharging for basic repairs.
relative
 noun
親戚、親族
A person connected to another by blood or marriage.
We gathered with all our relatives to celebrate New Year's Day.
